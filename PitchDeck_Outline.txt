SLIDE 2: THE PROBLEM
---------------------
Title: "Cricket Stats Lie"

Show two players side by side:

  Player A: 80 runs off 70 balls          Player B: 35 runs off 18 balls
  League match, team lost by 50 runs       IPL Final, chasing 175, 3 wickets left
  Traditional stat: "Great innings"        Traditional stat: "Decent cameo"

  CricketIQ Impact: 42 (Average)          CricketIQ Impact: 91 (Elite)

The point: Traditional stats treat both the same. CricketIQ knows the difference.

Key problems with current stats:
  - Runs/wickets ignore WHEN they happened
  - No credit for pressure situations
  - League match = IPL Final (same weight)
  - Bowling impact is basically ignored

SLIDE 9: WHAT MAKES US DIFFERENT
----------------------------------
Title: "Why CricketIQ Stands Out"

  1. KNOCKOUT AWARE — First metric to weight playoff performances higher
     (71 knockout matches detected across 18 seasons)

  2. DEFENDING PRESSURE — Bowlers defending 140 get rewarded for tight
     economy (no other metric does this)

  3. BOWLER PRESSURE — Bowlers aren't just "wickets taken." We model
     death-over pressure, defending context, and economy contribution

  4. ML-CALIBRATED — Not our opinion. Data from 1,169 matches decides
     what matters. Our guesses were wrong on death overs (1.5x vs 1.05x)

  5. FULLY EXPLAINABLE — SHAP breakdown for every player. No black box.
     Judges can verify any score.

  6. ROLLING FORM — Last 10 innings with exponential recency decay.
     Current form, not career stats.

SLIDE 11: LIMITATIONS (Shows honesty/maturity)
------------------------------------------------
Title: "What We'd Add Next"

  Current limitations:
  - No fielding impact (data doesn't have fielder names)
  - No venue adjustment (batting-friendly vs bowling pitches)
  - No team strength factor

  With CricHeroes data, we could add:
  - Individual fielding (catches, run outs, stumpings)
  - Venue-adjusted scoring
  - Opposition strength weighting
  - Real-time live match impact

=========================================
TIPS FOR PRESENTING:
=========================================

1. LEAD WITH THE PROBLEM (Slide 2) — The two-player comparison hooks
   the audience immediately. Everyone who watches cricket will "get it."

2. KEEP ML SIMPLE — Don't explain XGBoost internals. Just say "we fed
   1,169 matches into a computer and it told us our guesses were wrong."
   The comparison table (Slide 5) is your strongest ML slide.

3. SHOW REAL EXAMPLES — Slide 6 is where judges decide if your metric
   makes cricket sense. Pick examples that match intuition.

4. DEMO > SLIDES — If you can show the live app, do it. The SHAP
   waterfall chart and player profiles are impressive in person.

5. OWN THE LIMITATIONS — Slide 11 shows maturity. Mentors love when
   teams know what they'd improve.

6. TIME SPLIT (if 10 minutes):
   - Problem + Solution: 2 min
   - How it works + ML: 3 min
   - Real examples + Demo: 3 min
   - Differentiators + Closing: 2 min
